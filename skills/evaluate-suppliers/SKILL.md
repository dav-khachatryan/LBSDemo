# Evaluate suppliers

## Establish the decision
- Extract attendance or quantity, budget ceiling, fixed dates, required scope, and the selection rule from the brief.
- Treat budget, service capacity, date availability, and required scope as independent hard constraints when specified.
- Distinguish documented facts, interpretations, planning assumptions, and unresolved confirmations.
- Use only supplied facts when requested. Do not invent quality, reliability, service, discounts, extra capacity, or availability.
- Honour simulation boundaries. Do not contact suppliers, book, pay, or send messages without the appropriate explicit instruction.

## Calculate comparable costs
- Read the full proposal and terms, not just the headline total.
- Calculate billable quantity as the greater of actual quantity and a compulsory minimum, where applicable.
- Show quantity times rate, compulsory percentage charges on their stated base, flat fees, and total payable as separate steps.
- Apply each service charge only to its specified subtotal. Do not charge a fee twice.
- Preserve the stated tax treatment. Do not add tax to tax-inclusive amounts.
- Cite the proposal identifier and clause or section beside each material input or in a clearly mapped source block.
- Flag contradictory or missing terms. Do not represent an assumed amount as a confirmed payable price.

## Determine eligibility before ranking
- Check all hard constraints separately. Mark unknown availability as pending, not confirmed.
- Exclude proposals exceeding hard service capacity even if an arithmetic cost fits the budget.
- Label calculations beyond offered capacity as arithmetic only, not a valid service offer.
- Rank eligible suppliers by total payable when that is the supplied decision rule.
- If none qualifies, state that no proposal can currently be approved. Identify the exact constraint or confirmation needed.
- Permit conditional recommendations, but require confirmation of every hard constraint before approval.
- If attendance changes, recalculate costs and recheck eligibility. Never assume a supplier can expand a documented hard capacity limit.

## Prepare deliverables
- Use the available spreadsheet and document creation skills for requested file formats.
- Keep one compact comparison worksheet unless a distinct supporting schedule is necessary.
- Include editable planning quantities, typed numeric inputs, transparent formulas, total payable, budget headroom, capacity, date status, and overall eligibility.
- Separate the initial recommendation from any live scenario output. Do not let a static recommendation appear to update with edited inputs.
- Write a concise memo that leads with the selected supplier, evaluated quantity, total cost, and budget headroom.
- Explain material differences, conditional approval requirements, assumptions, and unresolved questions. Cite supporting proposals.
- Do not treat unknown operational details as new hard constraints unless the brief makes them mandatory.

## Verify
- Independently check headline totals, minimum billing and the service-charge base.
- Check boundary behaviour where quantity reaches or exceeds a minimum or capacity limit.
- Confirm that unknown date availability prevents approval even when cost and capacity pass.
- Recalculate, scan for spreadsheet errors, and inspect the rendered workbook.
- Render the memo and verify its requested page count and legibility.
- Save final deliverables through the environment's required persistence workflow.
